// Deepak Pandidurai

I study the invisible rivers that drown cities.

PhD researcher at IIT Roorkee (Hydrology Dept.) studying how anomalous atmospheric moisture transport — atmospheric rivers — fuels India's deadliest flood events. My work links monsoon dynamics, thermodynamics, and numerical modeling to answer one question: why do some storms flood entire river basins for days?

Before IIT, I was a Junior Research Fellow at IISER Mohali, running WRF simulations on an ISRO-funded project and publishing on extreme monsoon rainfall. I've presented at EGU Vienna, IAHS at IIT Roorkee, and IITM Pune — and co-organized the IAHS 2025 Scientific Assembly design vertical here.

Outside the lab: I sketch the landscapes I travel through, photograph what moves me, and run — competitively. Tamil is my first language; the atmosphere is my second.

Research & Experience

Current Focus 2024 → Present

Sequential Moisture Dynamics & Hydrological Extremes

How do atmospheric rivers chain together to produce multi-day flood-generating rainfall across India? I use HYSPLIT backward trajectories, ERA5 reanalysis, and WRF-ARW convective-permitting simulations to trace moisture from origin to flood.
